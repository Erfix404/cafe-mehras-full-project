# Cafe Mehras — Research Findings (2026)

## Sources
- createtoday.io 39 coffee sites (statistical)
- colorlib 27, sitebuilderreport 43, cyberoptik 20 (curated)
- Live: cultureespresso.com, ceremonycoffee.com
- 2026 web-design-trends reference (web-design-research skill)

## Hard data (39-site study)
- **66.7% light/near-white** backgrounds, only **18% dark** — the category norm is BRIGHT. Dark = deliberate differentiation.
- **Amber is the accent** on 47.4% — our saffron is on-trend but must be used as accent only.
- **76.3% photo-led heroes** — photography carries the site.
- **86.8% sans body**, headings may go decorative.
- Median nav = 4 items. Menu structure is lean.
- Muted palettes 66.7% — restraint over saturation.

## What the best cafe sites do (pattern synthesis)
1. **Hero answers immediately**: name + differentiator + hours + ONE primary CTA (view menu / order / visit). Legitimacy before scroll.
2. **Web-formatted menu** — not PDF. Text-based headings + descriptions (SEO + a11y).
3. **Story section** — sourcing, roasting, craft ("show the craft → builds loyalty").
4. **Photography-led** — real drinks, real space, people. Full-bleed sections.
5. **NAP + hours mirrored** with Google Business Profile. Trust strip (ratings/press).
6. **Sticky/order-ahead CTA** — online ordering integration separates best from rest.
7. **Locations finder** — for multi-branch.
8. **Instagram feed** — "Grammed" section keeps site alive.

## 2026 trend layer (what makes it feel NEW, not template)
1. **Bento grid** — asymmetric modular cards (menu highlights, story, hours in one scannable layout).
2. **Kinetic/display typography** — huge display type with tight tracking (Lalezar at extreme scale).
3. **Scroll storytelling** — timed reveals, sections as narrative beats (framer-motion whileInView).
4. **Ambient UI** — frosted translucent surfaces, layered warm shadows, no heavy borders.
5. **Micro-delight animations** — tactile buttons, quantity steppers, toast, shared-layout transitions.
6. **Warm near-blacks** (#12100E not #000) + ONE accent (saffron) — 2026 dark done right.
7. **3D/responsive element** — ONE interactive centerpiece (steam/mug), not full 3D.
8. **Reduced-motion + a11y** — non-negotiable baseline.

## Differentiation thesis for Cafe Mehras
Category norm = bright + amber + serif. **We invert**: dark-warm luxury + saffron accent + Persian arch motif. "قهوه‌ای که با قاب ایرانی سرو می‌شود" — the ONLY Persian-craft specialty coffee site in the field. Deliberate departure from the category, grounded in the memorable thing.
